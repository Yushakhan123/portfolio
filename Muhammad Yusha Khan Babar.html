Method

How I debug a hard problem.

Complex issues rarely have an obvious cause. I follow the same five steps every time so I am never guessing.

  1. Reproduce

    Make the bug happen on demand, with the exact steps and conditions.

  2. Isolate

    Strip away everything unrelated until only the failing part is left.

  3. Hypothesize

    List likely causes and rank them by evidence, not by hunch.

  4. Fix

    Change one thing at a time and fix the cause instead of the symptom.

  5. Verify

    Retest the original steps and the nearby features to confirm nothing broke.
